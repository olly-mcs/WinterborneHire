Pillars

A floral backdrop with serious impact.

Our floral pillars are one of our most versatile and popular ceremony looks, creating a beautiful statement backdrop with plenty of floral impact. They’re perfect if you want something abundant and impressive without a traditional arch.

Each pair is bespoke to your wedding, designed around your chosen colours, flowers and overall style. Go soft and romantic with delicate blooms, bring in plenty of colour for a wild and joyful feel, or choose a fuller, more dramatic design for a contemporary statement.

Choose two pillars at the same height for a classic, symmetrical look, or mix two different heights to create a modern broken-arch effect. Whichever style you choose, both pillars are packed with blooms for a lush, abundant finish.

Freestanding and easy to move, your pillars can frame your ceremony before being repositioned elsewhere at your wedding. Move them behind the top table, style your entrance, frame the dance floor or create another beautiful backdrop for photographs.

Our faux floral pillars are created using a mix of high-quality silk and real-touch flowers, giving you the look and abundance of fresh wedding flowers while staying beautiful throughout the day and beyond.

Questions

Are the flowers real?

No. Every design uses a mix of high-quality silk and real-touch artificial flowers, so it looks fresh all day and in every photo, whatever the weather.

Are faux flowers the same as artificial flowers?

Yes. Faux, artificial and fake all mean flowers that aren't real, but quality varies hugely. Ours are a mix of high-quality silk flowers and real-touch flowers, which feel soft and natural and look real even up close.

Can you match my colours?

Yes. Every piece is designed around your colour palette, flower preferences, venue and style.

Can I see my florals before the wedding?

Yes. Ahead of your wedding you'll be invited back to our showroom to see your arrangements mocked up, so you know everything is perfect.

How do I book?

Start with a consultation at our Dorchester showroom (opens in a new tab), where we'll talk through your colours, venue and ideas, or add the florals you love to your wish list and send it to us for a quote.
