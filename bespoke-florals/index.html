Statement wedding backdrops

Large floral pieces designed to transform your wedding venue.

From floral pillars and statement arches to elegant urns, plinths and custom installations, our collection of large wedding backdrops is designed to create unforgettable moments. Each piece is styled with bespoke faux florals in your chosen colours, so the look works perfectly with your venue and your wedding style.

01

Floral archways

A frame for the moment you say I do.

Our floral archways frame your ceremony beautifully, from a full arch of blooms to a lush foliage design or florals made to measure for your venue’s own arbor.

Every arch is designed in your colours, whether that’s soft whites and greenery or bright, joyful meadow blooms, and can move to frame your top table once the ceremony is over.

Custom installations

Florals for every corner of your venue.

When a space calls for something different, we design it. Hanging florals over long tables, dressed ladders and chandeliers, mantlepieces, pagodas and gazebos, churns and meadows along the aisle.

Each installation is made to measure for your venue and installed by us, so the whole room feels considered from the ceiling to the aisle.

03

Floral pillars

A statement backdrop without needing a traditional arch.

Our floral pillars are one of our most versatile pieces, creating a beautiful focal point for your ceremony or reception. They can be kept soft and romantic, filled with colour or styled with more dramatic blooms for a contemporary look.

They work beautifully at the ceremony, venue entrances, top tables and throughout your venue, and can be moved between spaces to make the most of them on your day.

Moongates

A classic shape, completely your own.

Our moongates provide the perfect framework for a stunning floral backdrop. Keep the florals concentrated for a modern look, create an abundant crescent or go for a totally foliage-led design.

Choose a fully floral moongate, or our metal moongate dressed with florals and draping in your colours.

Large urns & plinths

Elegant statement florals with a little more grandeur.

Our large urns and plinths are perfect for couples wanting to make an impact. They work beautifully in grand country houses, historic venues and formal ceremony spaces, but can also be styled with softer blooms for a more relaxed look.

Placed in pairs to frame your ceremony, they move easily to the reception afterwards to dress the top table or entrance.
