Bridesmaids' Bouquets

Your wedding party, all in bloom.

Your wedding party, all in bloom. Our bridesmaids' bouquets are smaller arrangements designed to complement yours and tie the whole party together.

Choose matching designs for a classic look, or give each bridesmaid her own colour from your palette for something more playful.

Like your bridal bouquet, they're made with high-quality faux florals, so they stay perfect from the first photos to the last dance.

For younger members of the wedding party, we also make single stem bouquets and fairy wands.

Questions

Are the flowers real?

No. Every design is made with high-quality silk and real-touch faux blooms, so it looks fresh all day and in every photo, whatever the weather.

Can you match my colours?

Yes. Every piece is designed around your colour palette, flower preferences, venue and style.

Can I see my florals before the wedding?

Yes. Ahead of your wedding you'll be invited back to our showroom to see your arrangements mocked up, so you know everything is perfect.

How do I book?

Start with a consultation at our Dorchester showroom (opens in a new tab), where we'll talk through your colours, venue and ideas, or add the florals you love to your wish list and send it to us for a quote.
