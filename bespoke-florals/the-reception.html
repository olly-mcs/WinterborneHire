The Reception

Bring your reception to life with flowers.

From beautiful centrepieces for your guests to a statement top table, our reception florals bring your wedding style into the heart of your celebration.

Choose from delicate bud vase clusters, low floral arrangements or taller statement centrepieces, all designed in your chosen colours and floral style. Your top table can then carry those flowers through for a beautifully cohesive look.

Designed to complement your ceremony flowers, our faux wedding florals, in high-quality silk and real-touch blooms, create a reception that feels considered, stylish and completely yours.

Bud Vases

Delicate details for beautifully styled tables.

Our glass bud vases are perfect for adding colour, texture and little moments of florals throughout your reception. Each vase is filled with a single stem or small selection of blooms, creating a relaxed and effortlessly pretty table setting.

Choose from clear, pink, green, orange or teal glass to complement your wedding colours and floral style.

Perfect scattered along long tables with candles and other details, or grouped together to add extra interest around a larger centrepiece. They’re a simple way to bring your flowers right into the heart of your reception.
