Questions

Are the flowers real?

No. Every design is made with high-quality silk and real-touch faux blooms, so it looks fresh all day and in every photo, whatever the weather.

Can you match my colours?

Yes. Every piece is designed around your colour palette, flower preferences, venue and style.

Can I see my florals before the wedding?

Yes. Ahead of your wedding you'll be invited back to our showroom to see your arrangements mocked up, so you know everything is perfect.

How do I book?

Start with a consultation at our Dorchester showroom, where we'll talk through your colours, venue and ideas. Prices are a guide; we'll confirm yours once your design is agreed.
