Arbors

Made to measure for your venue.

Many venues have their own arbor, pergola or gazebo, and with the right florals it becomes the most beautiful spot in the place.

We design florals made to measure for your venue's structure, so the design fits it exactly: a cascade across the top, blooms climbing the sides, or a full floral frame.

Every design is created in your colours and floral style, to tie in with the rest of your ceremony and reception florals.

Start with photos of your venue's arbor at your showroom consultation, and we'll design the florals around it.

Questions

Are the flowers real?

No. Every design uses a mix of high-quality silk and real-touch artificial flowers, so it looks fresh all day and in every photo, whatever the weather.

Are faux flowers the same as artificial flowers?

Yes. Faux, artificial and fake all mean flowers that aren't real, but quality varies hugely. Ours are a mix of high-quality silk flowers and real-touch flowers, which feel soft and natural and look real even up close.

Can you match my colours?

Yes. Every piece is designed around your colour palette, flower preferences, venue and style.

Can I see my florals before the wedding?

Yes. Ahead of your wedding you'll be invited back to our showroom to see your arrangements mocked up, so you know everything is perfect.

How do I book?

Start with a consultation at our Dorchester showroom (opens in a new tab), where we'll talk through your colours, venue and ideas, or add the florals you love to your wish list and send it to us for a quote.
