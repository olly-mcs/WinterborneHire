WEDDING STYLING MADE SIMPLE

Step 1.

Start by exploring our extensive catalogue of hireable wedding props & decorations. From vintage to modern, rustic to elegant, we offer a diverse range of items to suit various wedding themes to style your day. Book in to view our showroom space and discuss ideas with our in house wedding stylist.

Step 2.

Once you've found the perfect wedding decorations for your special day we can create your own bespoke quotation. You'll receive a confirmation email with a detailed breakdown of costs, including rental fees, delivery options, and any additional services you've requested.

Step 3.

To secure your booking, we request a 20% desposit which will hold your date and hire items. Then sit back and relax! We will be in touch a few weeks prior to your event to review your hire list

Step 4.

On the big day relaxing knowing that our team will handle the logistics, deliveries or wedding styling services booked.
Incorporate our beautiful props into your celebration and create unforgettable memories

Step 5.

After the event simply pack the props back into their original containers or packaging. Our team will arrange for a convenient pickup, ensuring a hassle-free return process or return to us at the agreed time at our showroom space.

Step 6.

After your event, feel free to share your experience with our wedding props and services. Your reviews help us continually improve and assist future customers in their decision-making process
