Our Services

Beautiful spaces, made simple.

However you choose to style your wedding, we’re here to help.

From full styling and setup, taking the stress away entirely, to hiring everything you need to style the venue yourself, or simply adding a few finishing touches, our services are designed to work around you.

With a beautiful collection of hire items and bespoke florals, we help you create a cohesive, thoughtfully styled day from start to finish.

Full Wedding Styling

Your vision, beautifully taken care of.

Want your wedding to look incredible without spending your wedding morning setting it all up? Our full styling service takes care of everything, from the first design ideas to the final pack-down.

Start by exploring our extensive collection of bespoke wedding florals and hire pieces, then work with us to create a considered look for your venue. We’ll help you choose the right florals, backdrops, table details, signage and finishing touches, making sure everything works beautifully together.

Once the design is finalised, we take it from there. We arrive ahead of your wedding to style and dress the venue, creating the finished look we’ve planned together.

And when the celebrations are over, we return to pack everything away.

You simply arrive, enjoy your day and spend your time with the people who matter.

Partial Wedding Styling

A little extra help, exactly where you need it.

Already planning to style most of your wedding yourself, but want us to take care of the bigger or trickier details?

Our partial styling service gives you the flexibility to choose where you’d like our help. Whether that’s setting up your outdoor ceremony, dressing your chairs or installing your floral backdrop, we’ll take care of the details you’d rather leave to us.

We’ll agree exactly what you need help with beforehand, so you can enjoy your day knowing those important details are taken care of.

DIY Wedding Hire

You bring the helpers. We’ll provide everything else.

Got plenty of willing friends and family ready to help? Our DIY hire service gives you the freedom to create your dream wedding, with everything you need from our collection of florals, styling and hire pieces.

Choose as much or as little as you need, from statement ceremony florals and centrepieces to chair drapes, signage, tableware and all those finishing touches.

We’ll have everything packed and ready to go, either for delivery straight to your venue or collection from our showroom. Once the celebrations are over, we’ll collect everything from your venue or you can return it to us.

The Process

Every wedding is different, and that’s exactly how we like it. We’ll work with you to create a look that feels personal, considered and completely yours.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Tell us about your day

    Your venue, your plans, your colours and the look you’re dreaming of.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Choose your style

    Explore our bespoke florals, hire collection and styling options, and decide what works for you.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Build your look

    We bring the pieces together, from statement ceremony florals and reception tables to signage and finishing touches.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Finalise the details

    We refine the colours, quantities and practical details so everything is ready for your wedding day.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Your wedding day

    Whether we style everything for you or you’re collecting and setting up yourself, we make the process simple from start to finish.
