Our Services

Beautiful spaces, made simple.

However you choose to style your wedding, we’re here to help.

From full styling and setup, taking the stress away entirely, to hiring everything you need to style the venue yourself, or simply adding a few finishing touches, our services are designed to work around you.

With a beautiful collection of hire items and bespoke florals, we help you create a cohesive, thoughtfully styled day from start to finish.

The Process

Every wedding is different, and that’s exactly how we like it. We’ll work with you to create a look that feels personal, considered and completely yours.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Tell us about your day

    Your venue, your plans, your colours and the look you’re dreaming of.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Choose your style

    Explore our bespoke florals, hire collection and styling options, and decide what works for you.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Build your look

    We bring the pieces together, from statement ceremony florals and reception tables to signage and finishing touches.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Finalise the details

    We refine the colours, quantities and practical details so everything is ready for your wedding day.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Your wedding day

    Whether we style everything for you or you’re collecting and setting up yourself, we make the process simple from start to finish.
