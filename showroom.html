Inside our showroom

An Aladdin's cave of wedding inspiration in Martinstown, just outside Dorchester.

Browse our beautiful range of props and chat through your ideas at our showroom based in Martinstown just outside Dorchester.

Our showroom is our much-loved Aladdin's cave and packed full of wedding inspiration and decorations to suit all styles of wedding day or event. We love to invite our couples along to view our range, talk through their venue decoration ideas and offer guidance on how to style their big day and create a wedding or event that is unique and personal for them.

Our showroom allows you to see ceiling decor, fully laid tables with centrepieces and other table settings, chair décor, backdrops, vintage rugs and much more. This allows you to envisage how everything will come together and what it will look like when set up on your wedding day.

We will go through what colours and styles you are looking to include and plan how to make the styling as personal to you as possible. We can review Pinterest boards or just chat through ideas and use our vast experience in design and styling to help you piece together all the essential items required to style your venue.
