Your own personal florist, with blooms that never wilt

At Winterborne Hire & Styling we create stunning faux floral arrangements that bring your wedding vision to life. Based in Dorchester, Dorset, we work with couples within a 50-mile radius, offering a truly bespoke service designed around your style.

Six reasons couples choose faux florals

Sustainable, long-lasting and allergy-friendly, and just as beautiful in person as they are in photos.

  1. 01

    Long-lasting

    Real flowers have a limited lifespan and can wilt over the course of the day. Faux flowers look fresh from the first photo to the last dance.

  2. 02

    Eco-friendly

    Fresh flowers carry a big footprint: transport, water, pesticides and fertilisers. Our blooms last roughly ten weddings, and we simply restyle them for each bespoke design.

  3. 03

    Allergy-friendly

    Faux flowers are hypoallergenic, with no pollen or fragrance, so they're kind to couples and guests with allergies.

  4. 04

    Versatile

    A huge range of colours, styles and sizes means your florals can match any theme or colour scheme for a cohesive, personal look.

  5. 05

    Consistent quality

    Weather, seasons and supply chains don't affect faux flowers. You get exactly what you chose, every time.

  6. 06

    Less stress

    No worrying about flowers arriving on time or in good condition. Your arrangements are taken care of, and you can see them mocked up in our showroom first.
