Why Choose Faux Florals for Your Wedding?

Faux florals are a fantastic option for couples looking for sustainable, long-lasting, and allergy-friendly alternatives to fresh flowers. With our high-quality artificial blooms, you can enjoy stunning floral designs that look just as beautiful in person as they do in photos. Plus, our hireable service means you can achieve your dream look without the cost or waste of fresh flowers.

6 Great reasons to choose Faux Florals

Long-lasting: Real flowers have a limited lifespan and may even wilt over the course of your wedding. Faux flowers, on the other hand will look fresh all day.

Eco-friendly: Real flowers have a large environmental impact, including carbon emissions from transportation, water usage, and the use of pesticides and fertilisers. Our faux flowers, on the other hand have a lifespan of roughly 10 weddings and do not require any water or pesticides to maintain their appearance. We simply restyle to suit each bespoke floral design.

Allergy-friendly: Real flowers can trigger allergies in some individuals, which can be especially problematic for couples or guests with severe allergies. Faux flowers are hypoallergenic and do not produce any pollen or fragrances, making them a great option for those with allergies.

Versatile: Faux flowers come in a wide range of colours, styles, and sizes, making them a versatile option for wedding decor. Our faux flowers can be customised to match any wedding theme or colour scheme, making them a great option for couples who want a cohesive and personalised look for their wedding.

Consistent quality: Real flowers can vary in quality and appearance depending on factors like weather, season, and more than even supply chains and inconsistent availability. Our faux flowers, on the other hand, are always consistent in appearance and quality, ensuring that couples get exactly what they want for their wedding day.

Reduced stress: Planning a wedding can be stressful, and one thing that can add to that stress is worrying about whether the flowers will arrive on time and in good condition. We help couples eliminate this stress by ensuring that their floral arrangements are already taken care of and will be perfect on their wedding day. Book a consultation with us and you can even see the designs mocked up in our showroom.

In conclusion, there are many advantages to using faux flowers over real flowers in weddings. They are cost-effective, eco-friendly, versatile, consistent in quality and can reduce stress for couples. With all of these benefits, it's no wonder that more and more couples are choosing Winterborne Hire & Styling to provide faux flowers for their wedding decor.
