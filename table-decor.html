Table Decor

Everything you need to style your tables beautifully.

The finishing touches can make all the difference. Our curated collection of table decor has everything you need to create a beautifully styled table, from elegant charger plates and candlelight to those little details that bring the whole look together.

Whether you’re styling a long wedding breakfast table, a collection of round tables or your top table, choose the pieces that work with your colour palette and style, and create a table that feels considered from every angle.

Choose your pieces. Add your florals. Create your look.

Coloured Napkins

A simple detail that makes a big difference.

Coloured napkins are a beautiful way to add colour, texture and personality to your table styling. Perfect for tying in with your wedding palette, they bring a considered, sophisticated look and help to finish each place setting.

Our linen-look napkins are available to hire in a range of colours, from soft neutrals to earthy tones and dusty pastels, so you can find the perfect shade to complement your florals, tableware and overall style.

Discover coloured napkins

Charger Plates

The perfect base for every place setting.

Charger plates frame each place setting and pull your table styling together, adding colour, texture and a little luxury before the first course arrives.

Choose from classic gold, a gold beaded rim, warm copper or natural seagrass, to suit anything from formal and elegant to relaxed and boho.

Discover charger plates

Gold Cutlery

A touch of gold at every place.

Our matte gold cutlery adds a soft, elegant shine to every place setting.

It works beautifully with our charger plates and coloured napkins, from classic and formal tables to relaxed and romantic ones.

Discover gold cutlery

Candlesticks

Warm candlelight for every table.

Candlesticks bring height, warmth and that soft candlelit glow to your reception tables.

Choose from vintage brass, cut glass, gold, silver, black or white, in classic or chunky styles, or make a statement with our acrylic candelabra.

Discover candlesticks

Bud Vases

Little pops of colour along your tables.

Bud vases are an easy way to add colour and character to your tables, with a single stem or a few small blooms in each.

Choose from coloured glass in pink, orange, turquoise and dark green, vintage cut glass or stoneware, or our assorted glass and stone bottles for a relaxed, collected look.

Discover bud vases

Tea Light Holders

A soft glow, table to table.

Tea light holders add a gentle, flickering glow to your tables as the evening draws in.

Choose from clear glass, gold rimmed, speckled, twine-wrapped or stemmed holders and glass cylinders, or hanging glass baubles to suspend above the table.

Discover tea light holders

Chair Drapes

Soft colour for every chair.

Our crinkle chair drapes add a soft, romantic finish to your chairs, tied simply at the back for a relaxed, flowing look.

Choose from five colours to tie in with your napkins, florals and overall palette.
