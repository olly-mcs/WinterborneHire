Your questions answered

Are your wedding flowers real?

No. We design with high-quality silk, artificial and real-touch faux flowers, so your florals look fresh all day and in every photo, whatever the weather, without the worry of wilting or seasonal availability.

Can we see our florals before the wedding?

Yes. After your design is agreed, we’ll invite you back to our showroom near Dorchester to see your arrangements mocked up, so you know exactly how everything comes together.

Can you match our colours, theme and venue?

Yes. Every design is bespoke, built around your colour palette, flower preferences, venue and style, from soft and romantic to wild and colourful.

Do you deliver and set up?

With our full styling service our team delivers, sets up your ceremony and reception, and packs everything down afterwards. If you’d rather style the day yourself, you can collect and return hire items from our showroom, or we can deliver them to your venue.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based near Dorchester and style weddings across Dorset and the surrounding counties. We’re recommended suppliers for Abbots Court, Abbotsbury Subtropical Gardens, Gorwell Farm and Kingston Maurward, and we have great relationships with countless other venues in and around Dorset.

How do we book?

Start by booking a visit to our showroom, where we’ll talk through your ideas. You can also add florals and hire pieces to your wish list and send it to us for a quote. Your date is secured with a holding deposit.
