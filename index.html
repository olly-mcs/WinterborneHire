      OUR SERVICES

      FULL STYLING SERVICE

      Our full wedding styling service gives you a totally stress free route to wedding decor heaven!

      Once we've agreed on your wedding vision simply leave it to us to liaise directly with your wedding venue to arrange set up and pack down of all of your hired items.

      Our wedding showroom in Dorchester is the best way to view all of our stock and try mocking up your dream table setup.

      DIY HIRE

      Our DIY approach offers a more informal approach to wedding styling.

      You can either collect and return your items via our Dorchester showroom. Or we can deliver them perfectly packaged to your wedding venue ready for you to set up in your own time before collecting them again once it's (sadly) all over!

      Our pick up / drop off DIY option is growing in popularity as it allows couples the flexibility to collect and return items at a time that suits them.

      BESPOKE FAUX FLORALS

      Our faux floral arrangements offer a cost effective way to bring the beauty of flowers into your wedding at a fraction of the cost.

      We create all kinds of faux floral wedding arrangements for various ceremony spaces. Such a top table arrangements or centepieces. We even do bridal bouquets!

      These can be provided as part of both our full styling and DIY options.
